# The Unwarned Night

# Humidity-Aware Nighttime Heat Risk Analysis

This repository contains the complete research implementation for
"The Unwarned Night", a research study focused on understanding
nighttime heat conditions and identifying situations where high
temperature and humidity combine to create dangerous heat stress.

The project uses climate and meteorological data to analyze nighttime
heat patterns, humidity conditions, persistence, trends, and potential
heat-risk events.

---

# Research Objective

The main objective of this research is to investigate:

> When and where do nighttime temperature and humidity combine to
> create potentially dangerous heat conditions, and how are these
> conditions changing over time?

The research combines temperature, relative humidity, heat-index
conditions, persistence analysis, and statistical trend analysis.
